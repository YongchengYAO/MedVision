## Reading these tables

- **Acc** — agreement on parsed samples. **AccCov** — the same with unparseable predictions counted as wrong; the gap between them is coverage.
- **κ / wκ** — Cohen's kappa and quadratic-weighted kappa (ordinal proxies only). 0 means no better than chance at the same marginals.
- **95% CI / p** — percentile bootstrap over whole imaging volumes, and a one-sided test of κ > 0 inverted from the same resamples.
- **⚠** on `Nparsed` marks fewer than 10 scored records: the statistic is arithmetically valid but practically meaningless.
- The decision-flip rate is omitted here because it is exactly 1 − Acc.
- κ is **not comparable across proxies** — it depends on how close the cohort's values sit to a cutoff. Compare models within a table.

## Decision agreement

Category of the prediction vs category of the ground-truth measurement. Both sides pass through the same cutoff table, so any disagreement is caused by measurement error alone.
